About

Hi, I'm Tammey.

Where creativity and technology meet, and where you can find your way forward again.

My story

I know how it feels to be stuck.

For years I felt stuck in a rut, unsure how to move forward, unfulfilled in my career and completely drained of creativity. Through self-reflection, personal growth work and finding the right resources, I was able to break free and rediscover my passion and purpose.

I grew up near Salt Lake City with a camera in my hand, then moved to Montana as a teenager and fell in love with hiking, fishing, archery and riding horses. After earning a technology degree from the University of Montana, I moved to Fargo, North Dakota, and built a career in tech.

To balance all that screen time, I took up scrapbooking. That turned into co-founding the online ScrapbookingClasses group, which grew to over 1,500 members, where I designed and taught new layouts every week.

Today I'm combining both sides: years of tech experience and a love of teaching.

Why Inspiration to Unstuck

You still have so much to give

I created this site for women over 50 who gave years to raising a family or to a career, and now feel exhausted, overwhelmed and all used up. You carry a lifetime of knowledge. You want your next chapter to have purpose, and maybe even leave a legacy. You just need a place to start.

Here you'll find journaling prompts, creative challenges, personal growth tips and friendly how-tos for tech and AI. It's everything I would have found helpful on my own journey.

"We all have the power within us to create the life we want. Sometimes we just need a little guidance, inspiration and accountability to take that first step forward."

I'm here to help you

  • Overcome limiting beliefs and self-doubt
  • Discover your true passions and purpose
  • Build the confidence to make big changes
  • Feel at home with technology and AI, so you can share what you know
