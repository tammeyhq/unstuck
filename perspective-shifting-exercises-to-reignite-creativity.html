Perspective-Shifting Exercises to Reignite Creativity

See familiar things in a new way.

By Tammey

As creative individuals, it's all too easy to get stuck in our ways, relying on the same perspectives and approaches time and time again. I hear you - it's comfortable, familiar, and a safe bet.

In celebration of National Skyscraper Day, I challenge you to embrace some perspective-shifting exercises that will breathe new life into your artistic practice and uncover hidden pockets of passion within. These exercises can help you see the world - and your creativity - in a whole new light.

Drawing Exercises to Shift Your Perspective

As artists, we have the incredible opportunity to shift our perspectives and view the world through a completely different lens. By challenging ourselves to explore new vantage points and techniques, we can unlock a wellspring of inspiration and breathe new life into our cityscape drawings.

  1. - Cityscape from Below Position yourself at the base of a towering skyscraper and draw the building from this unique vantage point. As you gaze upwards, capture the dramatic angles, the interplay of light and shadow, and the awe-inspiring scale of the architecture. This exercise encourages you to observe your surroundings with fresh eyes and find inspiration in unexpected places.
  2. - Architectural Abstraction Shift your focus from the big picture to the intricate details. Hone in on the captivating shapes, lines, and patterns found within the design of skyscrapers and other tall structures. Experiment with abstraction by isolating specific architectural elements and reinterpreting them through your drawings. Play with scale, repetition, and fragmentation to create visually striking, almost geometric compositions.
  3. - Imaginary Skylines Let your creativity soar by designing your own fantastical skyline. Blend realistic skyscraper silhouettes with whimsical, dream-like elements. Incorporate floating structures, futuristic designs, or natural landscapes woven between the towering buildings. This exercise taps into your imaginative side and allows you to envision cityscapes that defy convention.

Perspective-Shifting Journaling to Empower Your Creativity

You know, when it comes to our creative pursuits, sometimes we can all use a little perspective shift. As artists capturing the cityscapes around us, we have such an incredible opportunity to view the world through a completely different lens. And you know what? That same power of perspective can be a real game-changer in so many other areas of our lives as well.

Think about it - when you're feeling stuck in a rut, whether it's a personal struggle or a creative block, shifting your mindset and outlook can be the key to unlocking new possibilities. Maybe you're trying to heal from a past trauma or rebuilding after a broken relationship. Or perhaps you're just dealing with that all-too-familiar creative fatigue, where nothing seems to be sparking your imagination anymore. In times like these, embracing a shift in perspective can be truly transformative.

That's why I'm so excited about the thoughtful, exploratory exercises we have in store for you. These prompts are designed to get you curious, to encourage a bit of creative daydreaming, and to challenge your existing viewpoints in really meaningful ways. By stepping outside your normal way of seeing the world, you just might uncover some profound insights - about your art, about your life, about your very self.

So I urge you, don't be afraid to take that leap and let your perspective shift. Embrace the unknown, unleash your imagination, and see what kind of magic can happen. After all, the possibilities are as limitless as the skyline itself. All you have to do is open your mind and let your spirit soar. Are you ready?

Try tackling prompts like:

• If I could design my dream city, what would it look like? What elements would be important to include?

• What unexpected sources of inspiration have I discovered lately? How can I continue to seek out new and diverse sources of creative fuel?

• When was the last time I truly stepped out of my comfort zone in my art practice? What did I learn about myself in the process?

• How can I approach my daily life and creative routines with a greater sense of wonder and curiosity?

The key is to approach these journaling exercises with an open and exploratory mindset. Allow yourself to get curious, to daydream, and to challenge your existing perspectives. You may be surprised by the insights and inspirations that emerge.

So on this National Skyscraper Day, I encourage you to embrace a shift in perspective - whether through your sketchbook or your journal. Unlock new avenues of creativity, overcome fatigue, and rediscover the passion that first ignited your artistic spirit. The possibilities are as limitless as the skyline.
