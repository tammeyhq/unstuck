As a writer, one of the greatest gifts we can give ourselves is the ability to see the world with fresh eyes. To take the mundane, the overlooked, the everyday - and uncover the extraordinary hiding in plain sight.

It's so easy to get caught up in the routine of our lives, rushing from one task to the next without truly seeing the magic all around us. But what if we could recapture that childlike wonder - that innate curiosity that led us to while away summer afternoons searching for animal shapes in the clouds?

Whether it's envisioning the secret lives of household items, or transforming a mundane moment into a portal to another world, these prompts are designed to stimulate your creativity and encourage you to approach the world from a new perspective.

So get ready to let your imagination run wild! Who knows what kind of extraordinary stories might unfurl when you start seeing the world through fresh eyes. The only limit is your own creativity.
